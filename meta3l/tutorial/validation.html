meta3l validation

Four published three-level meta-analyses: the authors' metafor code and meta3l, on the same data

Nature Human Behaviour, open OSF data↔ authors' rma.mv code↔ meta3L(..., rho = 0)
4 / 4pooled estimates equal to the authors' code on the same rows, to the 4th decimal
4 / 4variance components and multilevel I² (between, within) equal
CR2robust intervals identical where the paper used CR2; up to 0.005 wider than model-based ones
rho = 0used to match the papers; the default rho = 0.5 moves the estimates by 0.003 to 0.02

The 0.15-point gap to the paper is already between the paper and the authors' own file (87 studies in the file, "k = 86" in the text). Events were rebuilt as proportion × n; meta3l accepts non-integer counts.
Why the intervals differ slightly meta3l always reports CR2 cluster-robust intervals (clubSandwich). Study 1 used CR2 and the intervals are identical; studies 2 to 4 used model-based intervals, so meta3l's are equal or up to 0.005 wider. The estimates and variance components do not depend on this choice.
